AvSGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Information We Collect

AvSGo may collect your name, mobile number, pickup and drop locations, device location, and booking information.

How We Use Information

We use this information for registration, vehicle bookings, connecting customers and drivers, fare calculation, live location services, and customer support.

Location Information

Location may be used to identify pickup locations and support driver location tracking during relevant services. You can control location permission through your device settings.

Information Sharing

Relevant information may be shared with assigned drivers, customers, managers, administrators, and service providers when necessary to provide AvSGo services. We do not sell personal information.

Payments

AvSGo may display UPI payment details or QR codes. Users should verify payment details before making payments through external payment applications.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information and retain it only as necessary for providing services and meeting legal requirements.

Children's Privacy

AvSGo is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings and contact us about your personal information.
